# Grok review #2 — Nov 2, 2026 (build cfd51ab, painted world live)

## Improved
- Painted world is the biggest upgrade: Thornwick and the overworld read as one cohesive painted world while keeping PS1 top-down readability.
- Menus/systems feel complete (party status, items/equip/status/quests/records, audio, text/battle speed, ATB mode, fullscreen, controls, save).
- Character voices are stronger; ambient flavor text adds personality without stopping play. Touch D-pad + A/B/MENU work.

## Still weak
- Movement feels sticky on the touch D-pad (step size, slow camera follow); hard to trigger an early encounter, so Ember gauge / telegraphs / stagger went unseen.
- Side content (3 towns, 4 optional dungeons, fishing, cards, arena) is not surfaced early.
- Personal quests and the Varra choice still read as setup, not consequence.
- Combat needs very clear telegraph + stagger language or the Ember gauge is "just another bar". Save reliability untested.

## Top 5 before Steam
1. **Combat clarity** — Ember gauge, boss telegraphs, stagger readable and satisfying in the first fights; players understand *why* a boss staggered; boss fights tense, not padded.
2. **Discoverability** — early NPC hints, map markers, a short quest with rewards leading to the new towns, optional dungeons, fishing, card game, arena.
3. **Narrative payoff** — visible consequences of personal quests and the Varra choice in dialogue, party reactions, endings.
4. **QoL / port** — save reliability + autosave, full controller nav incl. minigames, short Ember tutorial/tooltips.
5. **Consistency** — settings noticeably change things; ambient flavor text must not spam.

## Plan (this pass)
movement feel + early tutorial fight → (1) → (4) tutorial/autosave/flavor throttle → (2) → (3).
